About the Novel

A World Concealed in Shadows

Following the sudden, unexplained death of his father, Zian finds his ordinary reality shattered beyond repair. What initially appeared to be an isolated family tragedy quickly spirals into an intricate labyrinth of bizarre phenomena, cryptic warnings, and disturbing occurrences that defy logic.

Drawn into an intense detective investigation, Zian uncovers a terrifying secret: beneath the calm surface of everyday society lies a hidden world. A treacherous realm teeming with powerful curses, forgotten gods stirring after centuries of silence, and dangerous secrets buried in ancient bloodlines.

As malevolent forces close in and the whispers of darkness grow louder, Zian must unravel his father's forgotten past before the awakening nightmare consumes everything he holds dear.
